Company Description

Nexthink is the leader in digital employee experience management software. The company provides IT leaders with unprecedented insight allowing them to see, diagnose and fix issues at scale impacting employees anywhere, with any application or network, before employees notice the issue. As the first solution to allow IT to progress from reactive problem solving to proactive optimization, Nexthink enables its more than 1,300 customers to provide better digital experiences to more than 18 million employees. Dual headquartered in Lausanne, Switzerland and Boston, Massachusetts, Nexthink has 9 offices worldwide.

Job Description

We are seeking an AI research engineer with a hacker’s mindset, someone who thrives on rapid prototyping, creative problem-solving, and pushing boundaries. You’ll be part of our AI research team, taking ideas from rough sketches to production-ready systems that deliver real value. 

The ability to communicate complicated concepts in simple ways is vital as you will be working in a cross-functional environment. You will be expected to collaborate with other teams to integrate AI features into existing systems, as well as develop new applications. 

Responsibilities

As part of the AI team at Nexthink, you will: 

    Prototype fast to prove technical feasibility and value. 
    Transition promising PoCs into scalable, reliable AI solutions that solve real-world problems. 
    Stay up to date with the latest AI/ML advances and share knowledge with AI Nexthink community. 
    Communicate complex ideas simply, inspiring both technical and non-technical teammates. 

Qualifications

    BSc/MSc in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field 
    1-3 years of professional software-engineering experience, including internships or projects. 
    Solid background in statistics and deep understanding of AI/ML. 
    Basic experience in integrating AI models or LLMs (e.g., Prompting, Evaluation, Observability). 
    Hands-on, strong programming skills with experience in Python, in particular modern ML frameworks (langchain, scikit, pytorch, tensorflow, huggingface).  
    Excellent communication and collaboration skills in English (spoken & written). 

If you’re excited about joing one of the top companies working on AI and pushing the boundaries of AI we’d love to hear from you! Even if you don’t meet every requirement, we encourage you to apply—we value expertise, passion, and the drive to learn.
