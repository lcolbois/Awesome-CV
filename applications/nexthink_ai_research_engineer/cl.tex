\begin{cvletter}
After five years building and evaluating ML systems at the Idiap Research Institute, I am transitioning towards applied AI engineering, and the AI Research Engineer position at Nexthink is exactly the kind of role I am looking for.

My research background at Idiap directly maps onto the technical requirements of this role. During my postdoc, I built end-to-end evaluation pipelines for multimodal Large Language Models, covering prompt engineering exploration, development of automated evaluation metrics, and high-throughput inference optimization. As part of my work, I regularly apply MLOps practices such as configuration management, experiment tracking, and pipeline orchestration, building reproducible workflows which can support the path from prototypes to production-ready systems.  I also demonstrated rapid prototyping during a 9-day startup hackathon (Idiap Create Challenge 2025) in a team of three, where we built and delivered a working demo together with a pitch, winning first place. My core stack includes PyTorch, Hugging Face, and scikit-learn. I have not yet worked with LangChain or dedicated LLM observability tooling, but my MLOps background gives me a solid foundation to ramp up quickly, and I am always driven to learn new tools.

I have a track record of effective communication across both technical and non-technical audiences. Throughout my PhD, my work sat at the intersection of ML and forensic science, and I regularly presented to forensic scientists and law-enforcement personnel, translating complex AI concepts for audiences with an entirely different background and integrating their operational requirements back into my research. I also have experience teaching technical material through mentoring four Master's students.

In my previous position, my role centred on setting up research workflows and running model benchmarking experiments, while a dedicated team handled technology transfer when applicable. I am now looking to be more directly involved in bringing research prototypes to finalized solutions, and I believe the AI Research Engineer position would enable me to grow in that direction.

I am a native French speaker and fully fluent in English, and I hold Swiss citizenship. I am available to start immediately, and I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application in an interview.





\end{cvletter}
