\begin{cvletter}
I am applying for the AI Research Engineer position at EPFL as part of the Apertus Initiative. I am convinced of the necessity of public, open, sovereign Large Language Models as a healthy counterbalance against the few commercial actors currently dominating the field. Having always been invested in open science, and now actively bridging vision and language modalities through my work on vision-language model evaluation, I see contributing to the Apertus Initiative as the natural next step.

My postdoc at Idiap aligns most directly with the listed areas of model evaluation and benchmarking, multimodal AI models, and inference systems. I built end-to-end evaluation pipelines for open-weight Vision-Language Models (Gemma, Qwen, InternVL) in a face recognition context, focused on improving inference throughput using dedicated frameworks (vLLM), designed novel automated metrics to quantify model behaviour along several quality dimensions, and explored prompting strategies in depth. I have extensive experience working on Slurm-managed HPC environments.  The outcomes of my work focus on openness and reproducibility, as I always release my research code and take care to ensure the reproducibility of my research through proper MLOps practices such as configuration and environment management, experiment tracking, and pipeline orchestration. I have a track record of effective communication across both technical and non-technical audiences, which I expect will remain useful as Apertus continues to interface with the broader Swiss society. 

After several years of individually-driven research, I am especially drawn to Apertus for the chance to contribute within a larger collaborative team and to a model that is actively deployed and built upon, rather than only published.
I am a native French-speaker with full professional proficiency in English, and I hold Swiss citizenship. My postdoc contract ended at the end of March, so I am
available to start immediately, and I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application in an interview.


\end{cvletter}
