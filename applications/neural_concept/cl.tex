\begin{cvletter}

I am excited to apply for the ML Application Engineer position at Neural Concept. Your mission to revolutionize engineering workflows through the combination of 3D deep learning and physics-based simulation resonates deeply with my aspirations and background. Having studied numerical simulations and computational physics during my Master's at EPFL, and then specialized in machine learning during my PhD, I see Neural Concept as the perfect bridge between these two worlds: applying cutting-edge ML to solve real engineering challenges in aerospace, automotive, and energy. The role appeals to me because it combines technical implementation with collaboration, communication and knowledge transfer. 

My unique combination of skills positions me well for this role:
\begin{itemize}
\item\textbf{Engineering \& Physics Foundation:} My Master's in Computational Science \& Engineering at EPFL provided solid foundations in numerical methods (discretization algorithms, numerical solvers, FEM). While my recent work has focused on machine learning, I am eager to leverage this knowledge in an applied industrial context where ML meets engineering simulation. 

\item\textbf{Machine Learning Expertise:} My PhD and postdoc at Idiap gave me extensive hands-on experience with Python, PyTorch, and modern deep learning workflows. I developed a working knowledge of MLOps concepts (environment management, containerization, workflow orchestration) through my focus on research reproducibility. Having worked in my career with multiple modalities, I am comfortable adapting ML approaches to new domains.

\item\textbf{Communication Skills:} Throughout my academic career, I regularly had to communicate with a variety of technical and non-technical audiences. In the context of my research on deepfakes detection, I interacted with forensic scientists, police forces and legal experts, translated technical AI-related topics in a accessible way for them, understood their operational requirements and exploited these as feedback to better orient my research effort. More recently, I practiced pitching a technical startup idea to investors and customers at a hackathon (which my team won). I understand that effective ML application engineering is not just about technical implementation, but about effectively communicating with the customer and understanding their needs.
\end{itemize}

After five years in academic research, I am actively transitioning toward industry roles where I can have a more direct impact. I am Swiss, fluent in both French (mother tongue) and English. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my versatile profile, combining engineering foundations, ML expertise, and communication skills, could contribute to Neural Concept's mission. Thank you for considering my application.
\end{cvletter}
